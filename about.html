Our Mission & Standards

“Discover Better Nutrition for Every Paw.”

PAWFORM was founded with a singular purpose: to replace pet-food marketing confusion with transparent scientific education, culinary integrity, and independent creator perspectives.

Transparent Discovery

Why We Are Different From Pet Stores

PAWFORM is an independent content discovery platform, not an e-commerce retailer. We do not sell pet food, take kickbacks from industrial manufacturers, or promote products based on sponsored profit margins.

Our goal is to give pet parents the scientific literacy required to read guaranteed analysis panels, calculate dry matter protein, understand ingredient splitting, and explore fresh feeding wisdom.

Quality Control

Our Creator Standards & Ethics

Every expert featured on PAWFORM undergoes peer review based on four core pillars.

Scientific Rigor

Grounding all guidance in published veterinary biochemical literature and AAFCO/NRC nutrient minimums.

No Diagnostic Claims

We strictly avoid unproven medical or diagnostic claims. Nutrition supports general wellness, never replaces clinical care.

Ingredient Transparency

Demystifying byproducts, fractionated starches, render meals, and synthetic preservatives clearly.

Species-Appropriate

Respecting the distinct evolutionary physiology of obligate carnivore felines and omnivore-carnivore canines.

Editorial Integrity

Our 5-Stage Nutrition Verification Framework

How our editorial team and clinical veterinary council rigorously evaluate food topics, ingredient claims, and creator masterclasses before publication.

Stage 01
Biochemical Literature Check

Every claim is cross-referenced against peer-reviewed nutritional studies from the Journal of Animal Science, Veterinary Clinical Pathology, and NRC guidelines.

Stage 02
Dry Matter & Amino Acid Calculus

We calculate protein bioavailability on a 100% moisture-free dry matter basis, uncovering actual amino acid density vs raw ingredient water weight.

Stage 03
Zero Commercial Bias Firewall

Our editors hold zero stock or consulting contracts with corporate pet food brands. Content cannot be influenced by paid sponsorships.

Stage 04
Life-Stage & Species Validation

Formulas are categorized strictly by life stage: puppy skeletal growth calcium windows, adult maintenance, and geriatric metabolic MCT pathways.

Stage 05
Veterinary Council Peer Review

Before any masterclass goes live, certified DVMs review the visual and textual points to ensure pet safety and adherence to clinical guidelines.

Health & Nutrition Position Statement

PAWFORM provides educational information and is not a substitute for veterinary medical advice. Content published on this platform is for educational, exploratory, and general wellness purposes only. Never disregard professional veterinary guidance or delay seeking care because of something you have read or watched on PAWFORM.
